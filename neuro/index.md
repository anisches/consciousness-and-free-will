# Neuroscience and Agency

## Main Questions

- When does the brain prepare an action relative to conscious intention?
- Is conscious intention causal, interpretive, supervisory, or retrospective?
- How does the sense of agency arise?
- What happens when agency breaks clinically?
- How should neural prediction results affect moral responsibility?

## Initial Topics

### Readiness Potential

Libet-style experiments and later reinterpretations.

### Predictive Decoding

Studies attempting to predict decisions from brain activity before conscious awareness.

### Sense of Agency

Motor prediction, efference copy, comparator models, and intentional binding.

### Split Brain and Confabulation

Cases where explanation, agency, and action can dissociate.

### Disorders of Volition

Alien hand syndrome, anosognosia, utilization behavior, compulsions, and related clinical cases.

## Caution

Neuroscience can pressure naive accounts of conscious will, but it does not automatically settle metaphysical free will or moral responsibility. Keep those claims separate.
