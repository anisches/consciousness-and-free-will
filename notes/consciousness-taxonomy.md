# Consciousness Taxonomy

## Distinctions

### Phenomenal Consciousness

What experience feels like from the inside: the "what it is like" aspect.

### Access Consciousness

Information that is available for report, reasoning, memory, planning, or action.

### Self-Consciousness

Awareness of oneself as subject, agent, body, or continuing person.

### Meta-Consciousness

Awareness of one's own mental states as mental states.

## Major Theory Families

### Global Workspace Theory

Conscious contents are globally broadcast to many cognitive systems.

### Integrated Information Theory

Consciousness corresponds to integrated causal/informational structure.

### Higher-Order Theories

A mental state is conscious when represented by a higher-order thought or perception.

### Recurrent Processing Theory

Local recurrent neural processing is central to conscious experience.

### Predictive Processing Views

Conscious perception and agency are understood through prediction, error correction, and hierarchical inference.

### Illusionist / Deflationary Views

Consciousness as ordinarily conceived may be a cognitive illusion or misdescription.

## Open Classification Problem

The repo should separate theories of experience from theories of report, attention, memory, and action unless a source explicitly argues they are identical.
