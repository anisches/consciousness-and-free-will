## Idea
# Stress, Allostasis, and the Biology of Constraint

## Core thesis

Stress physiology changes the action-space before deliberation begins. Chronic stress, acute threat, sleep loss, hunger, poverty, and social subordination alter attention, impulse control, reward sensitivity, and threat detection. The chooser is not a neutral point floating above physiology.

## Why it matters for free will

A person under chronic allostatic load may technically face alternatives while having radically different access to inhibition, patience, trust, and future-oriented planning. This makes responsibility biologically graded rather than equal by fiat.

## Counterpoint

Constraint is not identical to compulsion. The hard question is how much control remains, how trainable it is, and what institutions can do to change the load.
