## Idea
# Navigational Uncertainty as a Trigger for Active Sampling

## Concept
Scanning behaviors and active sampling are directly modulated by the degree of navigational uncertainty and goal-heading deviation.

## Details
Detailed analysis of desert ant scanning confirms that the structure of the scan (the "shape" of the search) depends on:
1.  **Oscillator Phase:** The internal state of the LAL circuits.
2.  **Goal-Heading Deviation:** How far the insect is from its intended path.
3.  **Uncertainty:** The confidence in the navigational signal.
This reinforces the idea that agency in simple organisms is a manifestation of active inference processes managing uncertainty.
