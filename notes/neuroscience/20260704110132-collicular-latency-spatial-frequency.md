## Idea
# Collicular Latency and Spatial Frequency

## Mechanism
In the superior colliculus (SC), the time it takes for a neuron to respond (latency) is inversely proportional to the spatial frequency (SF) of the stimulus. Coarse, low-SF patterns elicit faster responses than fine, high-SF patterns.

## Independence from Tuning
Crucially, this latency effect in the SC is independent of the spatial frequency tuning curves of the neurons. Even a neuron that is tuned to high-SF will respond faster to a coarse pattern than to its preferred fine pattern, provided the coarse pattern is presented first or concurrently.

## Contrast with V1
In V1, latency is more closely tied to sensitivity; the neurons respond fastest to stimuli they are most sensitive to, regardless of whether they are coarse or fine.
