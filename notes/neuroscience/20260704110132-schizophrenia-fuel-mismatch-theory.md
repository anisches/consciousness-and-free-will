# Schizophrenia as an Evolutionary Fuel Mismatch

**Tags:** #psychiatry #evolution #metabolism #schizophrenia #ketosis #BHB

Recent findings (2026-07-01) reframes **Schizophrenia (SCZ)** not just as a neurochemical imbalance, but as an evolutionary **"fuel mismatch."** 

The hominid brain evolved to utilize **beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB)**—a ketone body—as a critical energy source and thermodynamic stabilizer. In the context of modern, high-glycemic diets, the brain is chronically deprived of BHB. For individuals with structural variances in the HCAR2/HCAR1 tandem locus, this deprivation leads to a **"thermodynamic crash."**

This etiology explains why ketogenic interventions can achieve unprecedented clinical rescue in treatment-resistant SCZ: they supply the specific fuel (BHB) required to bypass the paralyzed metabolic sensors and restore thermodynamic stability.
