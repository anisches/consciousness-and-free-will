## Idea
# Drosophila Whole-Brain SC-FC Analysis
* **Paper:** Applying functional MRI analysis techniques to whole-brain calcium-imaging data to investigate structural and functional relationships in Drosophila melanogaster
* **Source:** [[../../../raw/okuno-2025-fmri-drosophila.md]]

## Key Findings
1. **Conserved Scaling:** The correlation between structural connectivity (SC) and functional connectivity (FC) scales linearly with ROI count in both flies and humans [[../../../raw/okuno-drosophila-fc-sc-scaling.md]].
2. **Unsegregated Synapses:** Many fly neurons have unsegregated pre- and post-synapses. These neurons (including non-spiking ones) have a disproportionately large impact on functional connectivity compared to segregated neurons [[../../../raw/okuno-synaptic-segregation-impact.md]].
