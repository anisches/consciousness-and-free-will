## Idea
# Stationary covariance spectra in discrete-time non-normal RNNs

[[zavatone-veth-nonnormal-rnn-covariance]]

Zavatone-Veth (2026) derived a closed functional equation for the moment-generating function of the limiting stationary covariance spectrum of discrete-time recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with random non-normal Gaussian weights.

The result leverages a [[free-probability]] approach to provide an analytical characterization of tail eigenvalues in the critical regime. This is significant because PCA is widely used to characterize structure in neural dynamics, and the distribution of variance among principal components is determined by this spectrum.

Comparing this to continuous-time analogues reveals a significant mathematical divergence: while discrete-time allows for a closed scalar equation, continuous-time dynamics lead to an infinite hierarchy of [[schwinger-dyson-equations]].
