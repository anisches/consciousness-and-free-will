## Idea
# Computational Irreducibility and Undecidable Dynamics

## Core thesis

Some deterministic systems cannot be shortcut by a compact prediction. Their future behavior may be knowable only by running the system. Moore's work on undecidable dynamical systems and Wolfram's computational irreducibility both attack the fantasy of Laplacean compression: even if laws are fixed, forecast may be formally or computationally blocked.

## Why it matters for agency

- A deterministic mind may still be a no-shortcut process.
- "Could be caused" is weaker than "could be predicted by a smaller calculation."
- Deliberation may be the mechanism by which the answer is computed, not an ornament added after the answer exists.

## Counterpoint

Irreducibility does not prove libertarian free will. It gives epistemic opacity and process-realism, not uncaused choice.
