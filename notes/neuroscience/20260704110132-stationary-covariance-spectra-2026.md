## Idea
# Stationary covariance spectra of discrete-time non-normal random recurrent dynamics

- **Authors**: Jacob A. Zavatone-Veth
- **Date**: June 30, 2026
- **Source**: arXiv:2606.31944 [q-bio.NC]
- **Key Concepts**: [[non-normal-dynamics]], [[recurrent-neural-networks]], [[free-probability]], [[stationary-covariance]], [[spectral-theory]].

## Summary
This paper addresses the gap in understanding stationary covariance spectra for random non-normal recurrent dynamics. Using a free-probability approach, the author derives a closed functional equation for the moment generating function of the limiting stationary covariance spectrum in discrete-time RNNs with Gaussian weights.

## Key Findings
- **Discrete-time Characterization**: Derivation of a closed functional equation for the limiting stationary covariance spectrum.
- **Tail Eigenvalues**: Analysis of tail eigenvalue behavior in the critical regime, made possible by the closed-form characterization.
- **Discrete vs. Continuous Divergence**: Applying the same free-probability approach to continuous-time dynamics results in an infinite hierarchy of Schwinger-Dyson equations rather than a closed scalar equation.
- **Application**: Provides a theoretical framework for comparing models of non-normal dynamics to neural population data, specifically regarding PCA-based characterizations.
