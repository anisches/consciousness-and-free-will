## Idea
# Zhou & Hasler: Representational Geometry in Drosophila Visual System (2026)

This paper demonstrates that representational geometry can serve as a fidelity metric to distinguish connectome-constrained networks from arbitrary wiring.

## Key Findings
- **Biological Fidelity vs. Behavioral Fidelity**: Reaffirms that task performance alone does not guarantee biological realism.
- **Drosophila Visual System**: Using the Flyvis connectome-constrained ensemble, the authors show that circular direction tuning is a specific geometric property of biological wiring.
- **RSA/CKA Alignment**: Connectome-constrained networks match biological T4/T5 tuning substantially better than random baselines (Δr = 0.327).

## Connections to Agency
- **Representational Realism**: If we seek to understand the "goals" or "intents" of an organism, we must first verify that our models represent the world in a biologically faithful way.
- **Top-Down Constraint**: The connectome acts as a structural constraint that forces neural activity into specific geometric manifolds suitable for computation (e.g. direction estimation).
