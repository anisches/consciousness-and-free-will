# Kevin Mitchell on Emergent Agency (Summary of 'Free Agents', 2023)

## Core Thesis: The Evolution of Agency
Neurogeneticist Kevin Mitchell argues that evolution has produced organisms that are genuine **causal agents**. Unlike passive Rube Goldberg machines, living systems have "the power to shape their own destinies" through evolved biological mechanisms.

### 1. Top-Down Causation
Mitchell rejects strict bottom-up reductionism (the idea that molecules/neurons dictate everything). 
- **The Argument**: Biological systems are hierarchically organized. High-level goals (e.g., "I need food") exert **downward causation** on lower-level components (neural firing, muscle contraction).
- **Constraint**: The higher-level organization of the agent constrains the degrees of freedom of its parts to achieve a specific end.

### 2. Biological Meaning and Reasons
- **Meaning as Cause**: In living systems, information has *meaning* relative to the agent's survival. 
- **Reasons vs. Causes**: When an agent acts on a *reason* (a goal or a model of the world), that reason is a real causal explanation. Mitchell argues that the agent "settles" which possibility occurs, rather than being pushed by a singular prior physical cause.

### 3. Real Pluripotency (Alternative Possibilities)
- Against Sapolsky's determinism, Mitchell argues for **real possibilities**. Evolved neural systems allow for prediction, simulation, and deliberation. 
- The system is not just following a predetermined track; it is navigating a space of potential outcomes and actively selecting between them.

### 4. Evolutionary History of Volition
- **Simple Organisms**: Basic persistence and resource management.
- **Complex Organisms**: Nervous systems enable sensing, world-modeling, and "intrinsic causal power." 
- **Volition**: Emerges as the capacity to deliberate and choose among real alternatives to fulfill biological goals.

### 5. Critique of Sapolsky's 'Determined'
- **Reductionist Fallacy**: Sapolsky focuses exclusively on prior causes (genes, history, hormones) while ignoring the **integrated agent** as a causal force in its own right.
- **Incoherence of Language**: If "choice" is an illusion, the very concepts of "deliberation" and "reasoning" become scientifically empty. Mitchell argues these are real biological processes with causal power.

## Key Terms
- **Emergent Agency**: Agency that arises from biological organization.
- **Top-Down Causation**: Higher-level states influencing lower-level components.
- **Intrinsic Causal Power**: The ability of an organism to be the source of its own actions.
