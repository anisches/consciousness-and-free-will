## Idea
# Embodied Geometry and Affordance Spaces

## Core thesis

Agency is not computed in the brain alone. The body and environment define an affordance space: a geometry of reachable, salient, costly, forbidden, or invited actions. What an agent can choose depends on body schema, tools, terrain, social constraints, and learned skill.

## Why it matters

- Choice is constrained by what the agent can perceive as possible.
- The same environment has different action geometry for different bodies and skills.
- Niche construction lets organisms modify future affordance spaces.
- Embodied agency bridges neuroscience, ecology, phenomenology, and robotics.

## Counterpoint

Affordances explain situated possibility, not metaphysical freedom. They should be linked to specific perception-action loops and ecological constraints.
