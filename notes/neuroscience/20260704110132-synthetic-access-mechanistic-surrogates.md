# Synthetic Access and Mechanistic Surrogates

**Synthetic Access** is a governance-audited alternative to raw data access in neuroimaging research. It allows researchers to perform in-silico experimentation and generate synthetic cohorts without accessing individual-level raw data.

### Key Concepts
- **Mechanistic Surrogates**: The artifacts produced by [[cohort-amortized-personalization-cap|Cohort-Amortized Personalization (CAP)]] serve as surrogates that couple cohort priors to mechanistic simulators.
- **Privacy Preservation**: Because the shared artifact is a density estimator rather than raw data, it sidesteps re-identification risks and strict data-sharing regulations.
- **Utility**: Researchers can use these surrogates for:
    - **In-silico testing** of clinical interventions.
    - **Synthetic cohort generation** for power analysis or algorithm development.

This approach significantly lowers the barrier to entry for personalized modeling in clinical settings where data privacy is paramount.
