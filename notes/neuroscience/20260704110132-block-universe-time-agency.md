## Idea
# The Block Universe, Time, and Agency

## Core thesis

Relativity motivates an eternalist or block-universe picture in which past, present, and future are all parts of spacetime rather than a single moving present. The free-will question then changes shape: not "can the future be metaphysically open from nowhere?" but "can deliberation be a real causal process inside the block?"

## Agency inside the block

- From a God's-eye view, a decision may be part of a fixed worldline.
- From within the worldline, deliberation is still the process that determines which action occurs.
- Compatibilist physics treats agents as structured causal nodes, not exceptions to law.
- The feeling of flow may be perspectival, but perspectival does not mean unreal.

## Counterpoint

The block universe can make alternative possibilities difficult to interpret. The branch should distinguish psychological openness, epistemic openness, and metaphysical openness.
