## Idea
# Mitchell, Noble, and Top-Down Causation in Agency

## Core thesis

Mitchell's answer to Sapolsky is that biological organization matters. Living systems are not mere aggregates of bottom-up pushes; they are self-maintaining systems whose goals, models, and organism-level states constrain lower-level components. Top-down causation is not magic: it is constraint by organization.

## Why it matters

- Reasons can be biological causes when they are implemented by neural and bodily organization.
- The agent can be a real causal level without violating physics.
- Free will becomes evolved control over action-selection, not uncaused choice.

## Counterpoint

Top-down causation must specify mechanisms: constraints, feedback loops, control variables, and interventions. Otherwise it becomes a label for what reductionism missed.
