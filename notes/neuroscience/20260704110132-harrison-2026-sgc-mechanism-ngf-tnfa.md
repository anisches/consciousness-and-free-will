## Idea
# Molecular Mechanism of SGC-Mediated Homeostatic Plasticity

**Tags:** #glia #molecular-neuroscience #NGF #TNFa #cholinergic

The mechanism by which Satellite Glial Cells (SGCs) drive homeostatic plasticity involves a feedback loop with neuronal activity. SGCs respond to **cholinergic signaling** from neurons. 

In response to high activity (increased cholinergic signaling), SGCs downregulate the expression of the synaptic regulators **Nerve Growth Factor (NGF)** and **Tumor Necrosis Factor alpha (TNFα)**. This molecular downregulation by SGCs serves as a compensatory signal to the neurons to reduce synaptic density, thereby stabilizing sympathetic output during long-term changes in circuit activity.
