# Source: Applying functional MRI analysis techniques to whole-brain calcium-imaging data to investigate structural and functional relationships in Drosophila melanogaster
* **Authors:** Takuto Okuno, Alexander Woodward, Hideyuki Okano, Junichi Hata
* **DOI:** 10.1101/2025.07.01.662601
* **Year:** 2025
* **Tags:** #neuroscience #drosophila #fMRI #calcium-imaging #connectomics #SC-FC #non-spiking-neurons

## Summary
The paper applies fMRI pre-processing and analysis techniques to whole-brain calcium imaging data in *Drosophila melanogaster*. By comparing this data with EM-based connectomes (FlyEM/FlyWire), the authors investigate the parameters governing the relationship between structural connectivity (SC) and functional connectivity (FC).
