## Idea
# Universal Scaling of FC-SC Correlation in Drosophila and Humans
Functional connectivity (FC) and structural connectivity (SC) correlation decreases linearly as a function of the number of regions of interest (ROI). This linear trend is preserved across species, appearing in both *Drosophila melanogaster* (whole-brain calcium imaging) and humans (fMRI).

This finding suggests a fundamental topological constraint on how regional parcellation affects the observability of structure-function relationships. The scaling law implies that the apparent alignment between brain structure and function is highly dependent on the spatial scale of observation, and this dependency is a conserved feature of complex neural systems.
