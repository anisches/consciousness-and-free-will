## Idea
# Lorenz, Chaos, Determinism, and Predictability

## Core thesis

Determinism does not imply practical predictability. Lorenz's 1963 model showed that simple deterministic equations can produce nonperiodic behavior with sensitive dependence on initial conditions. For free-will debates, this blocks the naive move from "your action has causes" to "your action was forecastable in advance."

## Why it matters for agency

- A deterministic system can still be epistemically open to observers.
- Chaos gives unpredictability, not self-authorship.
- The right distinction is metaphysical determination vs. usable prediction.
- Human behavior may be lawful without being compressible into simple fate-talk.

## Counterpoint

Chaos is not freedom. A chaotic pendulum is not responsible. Agency needs control, learning, inhibition, goals, and self-modeling layered on top of unpredictability.
