# Quantum Disproof of the Principle of Sufficient Reason

The **Principle of Sufficient Reason (PSR)**, most famously articulated by Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, states that "nothing happens without a sufficient reason why it should be so and not otherwise."

## Conway's Challenge
John Conway argues that the [[20260704120200-conway-kochen-free-will-theorem-axioms|Free Will Theorem]] provides a scientific disproof of the PSR at the fundamental level of physics.

- If a particle's response is not a function of the past (as the theorem suggests), then there is **no reason**—no prior cause or hidden variable—that dictates why a particle chose "0" instead of "1" in a specific measurement.
- The outcome is truly spontaneous and uncaused by anything in the preceding history of the universe.

## Philosophical Implications
This represents a move away from the clockwork universe of Newtonian determinism. It suggests that at the base of reality, there are events that simply *occur* without being "reasoned" or "forced" by prior conditions.
