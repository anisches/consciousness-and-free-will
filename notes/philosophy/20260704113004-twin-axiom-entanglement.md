# TWIN Axiom (Entanglement)

The **TWIN Axiom** refers to the property of quantum entanglement where two particles are put into a "singlet state" (a state of zero total angular momentum).

## Definition
If two particles are "twinned" (entangled) and then remotely separated, measuring the squared spin of both particles in the *same* direction will always yield the *same* result (either both 0 or both 1).

## Significance in the Free Will Theorem
The TWIN axiom establishes a correlation between remotely separated events. In the proof of the Free Will Theorem, it is used to show that particles cannot simply "make up" answers independently if they are required to stay consistent with their "twin," unless they can communicate instantly (which is barred by the **FIN** axiom).
