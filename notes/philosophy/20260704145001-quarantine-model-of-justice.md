## Idea
If free will does not exist, retributive punishment is morally indefensible. Instead, society should adopt a "quarantine model" similar to how we treat people with infectious diseases. We can protect the public by restricting the movement of dangerous individuals (quarantine) without invoking blame or "evil." The focus shifts to containment, understanding the causes of behavior, and rehabilitation where possible.

## Why it matters
This reframing removes the cruelty inherent in many modern justice systems. It prioritizes public safety and the humane treatment of offenders over the "pleasure" of seeing people suffer for their "choices."
