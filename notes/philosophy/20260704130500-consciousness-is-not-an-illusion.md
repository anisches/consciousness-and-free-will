## Idea
While the "self" and "free will" may be illusory, consciousness (the "seeming" or "witness") cannot be. Any illusion requires a conscious observer to be "fooled." Therefore, the existence of an illusion is itself a proof of the existence of consciousness.

## Why it matters
It establishes consciousness as the primary, undeniable datum of experience, even if all the *contents* of that experience (like the feeling of being a "self") are false.
