## Idea
While accepting the lack of free will is intellectually and emotionally difficult, it provides the ultimate basis for compassion. If we recognize that everyone is the product of factors they didn't choose—genes, environment, trauma—it becomes impossible to truly hate someone for their failings. We can still dislike behavior and protect ourselves, but we lose the ground for moral superiority and dehumanization.

## Why it matters
This perspective can reduce social friction, hatred, and the "meritocratic hubris" that causes people to look down on those less fortunate.
