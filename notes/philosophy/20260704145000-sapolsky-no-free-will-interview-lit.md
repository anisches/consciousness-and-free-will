## Idea
In this interview, Robert Sapolsky discusses the implications of his book *Determined*. He argues that a scientific understanding of behavior leaves no room for free will and that accepting this should fundamentally transform our approach to criminal justice, meritocracy, and interpersonal judgment.

## Why it matters
The interview bridges the gap between biological theory and practical living. Sapolsky addresses the common fear that losing free will leads to nihilism or chaos, arguing instead for a more compassionate and rational society.
