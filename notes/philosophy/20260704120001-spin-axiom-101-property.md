# The Spin Axiom (101 Property)

The **Spin Axiom** (often called the 101-law) is an operational axiom in the context of the Free Will Theorem. It describes the observed behavior of certain particles (specifically spin-1 particles) when their spin is measured.

## The Rule
If you measure the squared component of spin of a spin-1 particle in three mutually perpendicular directions, the results will always be two $1$s and one $0$ (in some order).

- Mathematically, if the directions are $x, y, z$, then $S_x^2, S_y^2, S_z^2$ will always satisfy $S_x^2 + S_y^2 + S_z^2 = 2$.
- This property is "operational" because it can be verified in a laboratory through repeated experiments.

## Significance
This axiom is a cornerstone of the Kochen-Specker Paradox. It establishes a constraint on the particle's "responses" to directional queries. The paradox arises because there is no way to pre-assign $0$s and $1$s to every possible direction in space such that this $101$ rule is always satisfied for every orthogonal triplet.
