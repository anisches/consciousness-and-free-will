# SPIN Axiom (The 101 Law)

The **SPIN Axiom** is a fundamental property of spin-1 particles in quantum mechanics. It states that if you measure the squared component of spin of a spin-1 particle in three mutually perpendicular directions $(x, y, z)$, the results will always be a permutation of $(1, 0, 1)$.

## Key Properties
- The "squared component of spin" refers to the value $(\vec{S} \cdot \vec{n})^2$ where $\vec{n}$ is the measurement direction.
- For a spin-1 particle, the possible values of squared spin are $0$ and $1$.
- The sum of the squared components in any three orthogonal directions is always $2$.
- This result is independent of the order in which the measurements are performed, provided the directions are orthogonal.

## Importance in Free Will Theorem
The SPIN axiom provides the "101 law" which is essential for the Kochen-Specker Paradox. If particles were deterministic, they would have to "know" which directions will be orthogonal and have pre-determined answers for them. However, the Kochen-Specker paradox shows that no such pre-determined set of answers can exist globally for all possible directions.
