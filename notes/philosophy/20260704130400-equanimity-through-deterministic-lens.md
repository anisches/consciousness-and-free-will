## Idea
Self-hatred, shame, and regret are based on the false premise "I should have done otherwise." In a deterministic framework, given the same conditions, the same action would occur. Recognizing this allows one to treat errors as "actionable information" (data) for future improvement rather than as reasons for moral self-flagellation.

## Why it matters
It increases psychological resilience and "cruising altitude" in life by reducing the time spent in "deranging" states of mind like shame.
