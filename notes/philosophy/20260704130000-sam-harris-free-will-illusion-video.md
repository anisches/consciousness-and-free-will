## Idea
Sam Harris argues that free will is not just an illusion, but that the *experience* of having it is also an illusion upon close inspection. He connects the absence of free will to the absence of a stable "self" and discusses the ethical implications of adopting a deterministic view, specifically regarding compassion, hatred, and self-forgiveness.

## Why it matters
This perspective shifts the foundation of moral responsibility from ultimate authorship to the practical management of consequences and well-being. It provides a psychological toolkit for reducing hatred and regret.
