# Compatibility of General Relativity and Free Choice

General Relativity is often perceived as a "block universe" where everything is pre-determined by the geometry of space-time. However, John Conway argues that the theory is fully compatible with free will.

The key insight is that the distribution of mass determines the shape of the universe. If humans possess free will, their actions can change the distribution of mass (e.g., Theodore Roosevelt's decision to build the Panama Canal involved moving massive amounts of earth). 

Therefore:
- The shape of the universe is not pre-fixed but is a result of free decisions.
- Space-time geometry only "fixes" after the events have occurred.
- We are not "dragged" across a pre-existing shape; rather, the shape is formed by the path we choose to take.

This perspective integrates physical laws with agency, suggesting that the four-dimensional manifold of the universe is a record of free choices rather than a deterministic script.
