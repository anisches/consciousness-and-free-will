# SPIN Axiom (The 101 Rule)

In the context of the Free Will Theorem, the **SPIN Axiom** is an operationally decidable property of spin-1 particles.

## Definition
When the squared component of spin for a spin-1 particle is measured in three mutually perpendicular (orthogonal) directions, the results will always consist of two '1's and one '0', in some order.

## Significance
This "101 Rule" is a fundamental constraint on particle behavior. The **Kochen-Specker Paradox** demonstrates that it is mathematically impossible to pre-assign a set of 1s and 0s to all possible measurement directions such that every orthogonal triplet satisfies this rule. This implies that the particle cannot have "fixed" answers for every possible measurement direction before the measurement occurs.
