- **Date Ingested**: 2026-07-04

## Summary
In this second lecture, John Conway provides a detailed proof of the Kochen-Specker theorem using a configuration of 33 directions. He argues that the squared spin components of a spin-1 particle do not have predetermined values (hidden variables) before measurement. This leads to the "Free Will Theorem," which states that if experimenters have free will in choosing measurement directions, then the particles themselves must also exhibit "free will" in their responses (meaning their responses are not a function of the past).
