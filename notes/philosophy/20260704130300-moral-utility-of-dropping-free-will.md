## Idea
Hatred is often anchored in the belief that an agent is the ultimate, free author of their actions. By recognizing that people are products of their biology, environment, and "luck" (genetic and situational), we can view harmful agents similarly to forces of nature or malfunctioning machines. This allows for opposition to harm without the "poison" of personal hatred.

## Why it matters
It offers a path to "radical forgiveness" and compassion that is based on reason and a realistic view of causation rather than just religious or moral exhortation.
