## Idea
Thoughts and intentions arise in consciousness without the conscious "self" knowing what they will be until they arrive. Since you cannot know your next thought before you think it, you cannot be its author in any meaningful sense. You are simply the space in which the thought appears.

## Why it matters
This realization undercuts the ego's claim to ultimate responsibility for "good" or "bad" ideas, shifting focus to the quality of the stream of consciousness itself.

## Open questions
- Can we consciously curate the *environment* that generates thoughts, even if we don't author the thoughts themselves?
