# FIN Axiom (The Speed of Light Limit)

The **FIN Axiom** is derived from the Special Theory of Relativity and is central to the proof of the Free Will Theorem.

## Definition
There is a finite maximum speed at which information can be transmitted between two points in the universe (the speed of light, $c$).

## Logic
In relativity, there is no absolute notion of time or simultaneity. If information could travel faster than light, it would be possible to transmit information backward in time from some coordinate frames. This would lead to causal paradoxes, such as an effect occurring before its cause.

## Role in Free Will
The FIN axiom prevents two remotely separated "twinned" particles from communicating their measurement settings or results to each other faster than the speed of light. This forces the conclusion that if their responses are correlated (TWIN) and not pre-determined (SPIN), they must be generated by a "free" choice at the moment of measurement.
