# Quantum Mechanics, Time, and Agency

Intersection of fundamental physics and the philosophy of action. This branch should be disciplined: quantum indeterminacy can break classical predictability, but randomness is not agency. The interesting questions are whether physics permits genuine openness, whether the block universe erases becoming, and whether causal structure can host agents without metaphysical magic.

## Core Branches

### Quantum Indeterminacy Is Not Yet Free Will
*Wave-function collapse, Born probabilities, and quantum randomness can undercut strict classical determinism, but random outcomes are not authored choices.* Any serious quantum-free-will argument must explain control, not merely indeterminacy.

### Decoherence and the Warm-Brain Problem
*Quantum effects are real everywhere, but robust cognitive-level quantum computation in the brain is controversial.* Penrose/Hameroff-style Orch-OR belongs here as a speculative bridge, paired with decoherence critiques.

### The Free Will Theorem
*Conway and Kochen argue that if experimenters have a certain kind of freedom in choosing measurement settings, then elementary particles have a corresponding non-deterministic response.* This is not human free will, but it exposes how physics smuggles assumptions about experimental choice into foundations.

### Superdeterminism and the Cost of Closing Every Door
*Superdeterminism preserves determinism by denying independent measurement settings.* It is a serious loophole in Bell-style reasoning, but it risks making scientific independence and experimental intervention harder to interpret.

### The Block Universe and the Experience of Flow
*Relativity encourages an eternalist picture where past, present, and future coexist in spacetime geometry.* The agency question becomes: can deliberation be real inside a block, or is "choosing" merely how a worldline feels from within?

### Causal Structure Without Contra-Causality
*Agents may be real causal nodes without being uncaused causes.* Interventionist and compatibilist framings let physics host agency as organized causal structure rather than metaphysical exception.
