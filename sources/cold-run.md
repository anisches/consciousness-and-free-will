# Cold Run Source Map

This is the first source map for the project. It is not a thesis. It is a scaffold for collecting papers, books, public essays, and neuroscience results before taking a position.

## Classification Rules

- Prefer primary papers, serious reviews, and stable encyclopedic entries.
- Keep peer-reviewed sources separate from public essays and blogs.
- Mark links as `verify` when the bibliographic trail is not yet clean.
- Do not let any single example, including Libet-style experiments, dominate the map.
- Separate consciousness, agency, responsibility, and moral blame unless a source explicitly connects them.

## Historical and Philosophical Primitives

### Aristotle, _Nicomachean Ethics_, Book III

- Link: https://classics.mit.edu/Aristotle/nicomachaen.3.iii.html
- Question: How do voluntary action, choice, compulsion, and ignorance affect responsibility?
- Role: Early anchor for voluntary action and ethical responsibility.
- Type: Primary text
- Status: To read

### Augustine, _On Free Choice of the Will_

- Link: https://archive.org/details/onfreechoiceofwi0000augu
- Question: How can will, evil, divine order, and responsibility coexist?
- Role: Theological and moral framing of free choice.
- Type: Primary text
- Status: To read

### David Hume, _An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding_, Section VIII

- Link: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/9662/9662-h/9662-h.htm#section8
- Question: Can liberty and necessity be reconciled?
- Role: Classic compatibilist framing.
- Type: Primary text
- Status: To read

### Harry Frankfurt, "Alternate Possibilities and Moral Responsibility"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.2307/2023833
- Question: Is alternate possibility required for moral responsibility?
- Role: Introduces Frankfurt-style cases against the principle of alternate possibilities.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### P. F. Strawson, "Freedom and Resentment"

- Link: https://www.homepages.ucl.ac.uk/~uctytho/dfwstrawson1.htm
- Question: Should moral responsibility be grounded in metaphysics or in reactive attitudes?
- Role: Major compatibilist/practice-based account of responsibility.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### Peter van Inwagen, _An Essay on Free Will_

- Link: https://global.oup.com/academic/product/an-essay-on-free-will-9780198249245
- Question: Does determinism rule out the ability to do otherwise?
- Role: Source for the consequence argument and modern incompatibilism.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

### John Martin Fischer and Mark Ravizza, _Responsibility and Control_

- Link: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/responsibility-and-control/7EBCCB84E22007F4CDAF73A0AB3F663A
- Question: Can responsibility survive without alternative possibilities?
- Role: Reasons-responsive compatibilist control account.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

### Robert Kane, _The Significance of Free Will_

- Link: https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-significance-of-free-will-9780195126563
- Question: Can libertarian free will be made naturalistic?
- Role: Major contemporary libertarian account.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

### Derk Pereboom, _Living Without Free Will_

- Link: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/living-without-free-will/77E46D7BA53081DB897602922E0B7E20
- Question: What follows if hard incompatibilism is true?
- Role: Skeptical account of desert-based responsibility.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

## Consciousness Theories

### Thomas Nagel, "What Is It Like to Be a Bat?"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.2307/2183914
- Question: What is subjective character?
- Role: Anchor for the first-person character of experience.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia"

- Link: https://www.jstor.org/stable/2960077
- Question: Does complete physical knowledge leave something out?
- Role: Source for Mary the color scientist.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### David Chalmers, "Facing Up to the Problem of Consciousness"

- Link: https://consc.net/papers/facing.html
- Question: What distinguishes the hard problem from the easy problems?
- Role: Establishes the hard-problem framing.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### Ned Block, "On a Confusion About a Function of Consciousness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00038188
- Question: What is the difference between phenomenal consciousness and access consciousness?
- Role: Core distinction for avoiding category errors.
- Type: Philosophical argument
- Status: To read

### Daniel Dennett, _Consciousness Explained_

- Link: https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/28790/consciousness-explained-by-daniel-c-dennett/
- Question: Can consciousness be explained without a Cartesian theater?
- Role: Multiple-drafts and deflationary account.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

### Bernard Baars, _A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness_

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511550768
- Question: How does global availability explain conscious access?
- Role: Foundational global workspace account.
- Type: Book
- Status: To read

### Dehaene and Naccache, "Towards a Cognitive Neuroscience of Consciousness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0010-0277%2800%2900123-2
- Question: What neural and cognitive architecture supports conscious access?
- Role: Early global neuronal workspace synthesis.
- Type: Review / theory paper
- Status: To read

### Mashour et al., "Conscious Processing and the Global Neuronal Workspace Hypothesis"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2020.01.026
- Question: What is the current GNW account of conscious processing?
- Role: Contemporary review and update of GNW.
- Type: Review
- Status: To read

### Tononi, "An Information Integration Theory of Consciousness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1186/1471-2202-5-42
- Question: Can consciousness be formalized as integrated information?
- Role: Early IIT anchor.
- Type: Theory paper
- Status: To read

### Oizumi, Albantakis, and Tononi, "From the Phenomenology to the Mechanisms of Consciousness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003588
- Question: How does IIT 3.0 connect phenomenology to mechanisms?
- Role: Later formal IIT anchor.
- Type: Theory paper
- Status: To read

### Lau and Rosenthal, "Empirical Support for Higher-Order Theories of Conscious Awareness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2011.05.009
- Question: Are conscious states conscious because of higher-order representation?
- Role: Higher-order theory bridge between philosophy and cognitive neuroscience.
- Type: Review / theory paper
- Status: To read

### Lamme, "Towards a True Neural Stance on Consciousness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2006.09.001
- Question: Is recurrent processing enough for visual awareness?
- Role: Recurrent-processing alternative to access/report-heavy accounts.
- Type: Review / theory paper
- Status: To read

## Neuroscience of Agency and Volition

### Libet et al., "Time of Conscious Intention to Act in Relation to Onset of Cerebral Activity"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1093/brain/106.3.623
- Question: When does brain preparation precede reported conscious intention?
- Role: Classic readiness-potential study. Important, but only one piece of the neuroscience map.
- Type: Empirical study
- Status: To read

### Soon, Brass, Heinze, and Haynes, "Unconscious Determinants of Free Decisions in the Human Brain"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.2112
- Question: Can choices be decoded from brain activity before reported awareness?
- Role: fMRI decoding study often cited in free-will debates.
- Type: Empirical study
- Status: To read

### Schurger, Sitt, and Dehaene, "An Accumulator Model for Spontaneous Neural Activity Prior to Self-Initiated Movement"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1210467109
- Question: Could readiness potential reflect stochastic accumulation rather than a settled unconscious decision?
- Role: Major reinterpretation of Libet-style readiness potentials.
- Type: Empirical / theory paper
- Status: To read

### Haggard, "Sense of Agency in the Human Brain"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2017.14
- Question: How does the brain generate the sense of agency?
- Role: Review anchor for intention, action, and agency experience.
- Type: Review
- Status: To read

### Wegner and Wheatley, "Apparent Mental Causation"

- Link: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10424155/
- Question: How do people infer that conscious thoughts caused actions?
- Role: Psychological account of conscious will as attribution/inference.
- Type: Empirical / theory paper
- Status: To read

### Moore and Obhi, "Intentional Binding and the Sense of Agency"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2012.00179
- Question: What does intentional binding measure?
- Role: Review of an experimental marker often used in agency research.
- Type: Review
- Status: To read

### Sperry, "Hemisphere Deconnection and Unity in Conscious Awareness"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026839
- Question: What do split-brain cases imply about unity of consciousness?
- Role: Clinical/experimental pressure on simple unified-agent pictures.
- Type: Empirical / clinical review
- Status: To read

### Gazzaniga, "Forty-Five Years of Split-Brain Research and Still Going Strong"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2015.8
- Question: What has split-brain work shown about explanation, agency, and unity?
- Role: Modern review of split-brain implications.
- Type: Review
- Status: To read

### Frith, Blakemore, and Wolpert, "Abnormalities in the Awareness and Control of Action"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2000.0734
- Question: What happens when action control and awareness dissociate?
- Role: Clinical route into schizophrenia, comparator models, and agency disturbance.
- Type: Review / clinical neuroscience
- Status: To read

### Biran and Chatterjee, "Alien Hand Syndrome"

- Link: https://doi.org/10.1001/archneur.61.2.292
- Question: What does alien hand syndrome show about action ownership and control?
- Role: Clinical case family for dissociations in agency.
- Type: Clinical review
- Status: To read

## Thought Experiments and Boundary Cases

### Mary the Color Scientist

- Anchor: Frank Jackson, "Epiphenomenal Qualia"
- Link: https://www.jstor.org/stable/2960077
- Tests: Whether physical knowledge leaves out phenomenal knowledge.
- Status: To classify

### Philosophical Zombies

- Anchor: David Chalmers, _The Conscious Mind_
- Link: https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-conscious-mind-9780195117899
- Tests: Whether consciousness is logically separable from physical/functionalist description.
- Status: To classify

### Chinese Room

- Anchor: John Searle, "Minds, Brains, and Programs"
- Link: https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00005756
- Tests: Whether symbol manipulation is sufficient for understanding.
- Status: To classify

### Frankfurt Cases

- Anchor: Frankfurt, "Alternate Possibilities and Moral Responsibility"
- Link: https://doi.org/10.2307/2023833
- Tests: Whether responsibility requires alternative possibilities.
- Status: To classify

### Manipulation Cases

- Anchor: Derk Pereboom's four-case argument
- Link: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/living-without-free-will/77E46D7BA53081DB897602922E0B7E20
- Tests: Whether causal history undermines sourcehood and responsibility.
- Status: To classify

### Split Brain

- Anchor: Sperry and Gazzaniga split-brain research
- Links: https://doi.org/10.1037/h0026839 and https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn.2015.8
- Tests: Whether agency and consciousness are unified in the way ordinary introspection suggests.
- Status: To classify
