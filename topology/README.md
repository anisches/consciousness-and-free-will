# The Topology & Geometry of Experience

Mapping consciousness and agency onto state spaces, neural manifolds, attractors, and embodied geometries. The point is not to make qualia sound mathematical; it is to ask whether the shape of a system's possible states constrains what it can experience, decide, remember, and become.

## Core Branches

### Neural Manifolds and Low-Dimensional Thought
*Population activity lives in constrained low-dimensional manifolds rather than filling all possible neural states.* Decision and experience can be studied as trajectories through structured state spaces. In non-normal recurrent networks, the stationary covariance spectrum (which determines PCA structure) can be analytically characterized in discrete time [[../raw/zavatone-veth-nonnormal-rnn-covariance.md]], revealing critical differences between discrete and continuous-time dynamics [[../raw/non-normal-discrete-continuous-divergence.md]]. Further, mean-field theory shows that low-rank structures combined with adaptation produce coherent population oscillations that coexist with single-neuron chaos [[../raw/chaos-coherence-coexistence.md]]. Crucially, representational geometry (pairwise population distances) provides a fidelity metric to verify whether connectome-constrained manifolds align with biological computation, such as circular direction geometry in the Drosophila visual system [[../biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/zhou_flyvis_geometry_2026.md]]. This geometric constraint is leveraged in **Cohort-Amortized Personalization (CAP)**, which uses low-rank cohort priors and neural density estimators to map site-specific connectomes into shared latent spaces for personalized brain modeling [[../biology/neuroscience/computation/esmaeili_cap_brain_twins_2026.md]]. The **DRIADA toolkit** further extends this by providing a unified Python framework for cross-scale analysis, demonstrating how selectivity-based filtering can restore latent embeddings (e.g., hippocampal spatial maps) and revealing that ~57% of manifold-informative neurons are not selective for measured behavioral features [[../biology/neuroscience/computation/pospelov_driada_toolkit_2026.md]].

### Attractors, Basins, and Habitual Selves
*Habits, moods, identities, and obsessions can be modeled as attractor basins.* Agency becomes the ability to reshape the landscape, escape a basin, or change the transitions between states.

### Metastability and the Edge of Choice
*Brains often hover between stable regimes, neither frozen nor random.* Metastable dynamics may explain why deliberation feels like being suspended among possible actions before one trajectory wins.

### Non-Euclidean Consciousness
*Memory, salience, trauma, and anticipation warp subjective distance.* Two events may be objectively far apart but phenomenologically adjacent; topology may matter more than clock time.

### Fractals and Infinite Regress
*Introspection can recurse: a self modeling a self modeling a self.* Fractal metaphors are useful when they illuminate scale-invariant loops, but dangerous when they become decorative mysticism.

### Linear Algebra of Subjective Experience
*Qualia and concepts can be modeled as vectors or regions in representational spaces.* The open question is whether geometry captures only reportable structure or something about experience itself.

### Embodied and Ecological Geometry
*The action-space is shaped by the body and environment, not just the brain.* Affordances, motor constraints, and niche geometry determine which "choices" are even reachable. In desert ants, active sampling emerges from the geometry of goal-heading deviation and internal oscillators [[../biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/ant_scanning_circuits_2026.md]]. Longitudinal human eye-tracking further demonstrates that systematic drift in perceptual sampling maps (fixation density) is sufficient to drive representational drift in hierarchical visual models (CORnet-S), suggesting that behavioral shifts are a primary source of neural state-space instability [[../biology/neuroscience/computation/perceptual_sampling_drift_2026.md]].
