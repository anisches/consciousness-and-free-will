# Biological Agency and Animal Models

Empirical evidence for agency across living systems: genetics, development, hormones, stress, learning, ecological affordances, and neural control loops. Biology is where the free-will debate stops being a slogan: Sapolsky is strongest here because he can show the causal chain entering the body; Mitchell is strongest here because organisms are not passive billiard balls but self-maintaining systems with goals, feedback, and evolved control.

## Core Branches

### Sapolsky's Causal Cascade
*Every action has a history: milliseconds of neural firing, minutes of hormones, years of development, and millennia of evolution.* Sapolsky's hard determinism is not "genes made me do it" but a multi-scale causal stack in which no isolated inner self escapes biology.

### Stress, Allostasis, and the Biology of Constraint
*Stress physiology changes the available action-space before deliberation begins.* Glucocorticoids, threat states, poverty, trauma, sleep, hunger, and social rank shift attention, impulse control, and reward valuation — making responsibility biologically uneven.

### Development, Plasticity, and Biological Luck
*The chooser is built before it chooses.* Early-life adversity, epigenetic regulation, attachment, learning history, and neuroplasticity shape later self-control and temperament. Harrison et al. (2026) [[../raw/harrison-2026-sgc-homeostatic-plasticity.md]] provide a concrete mechanism for this in the peripheral nervous system, showing how glial cells (SGCs) drive homeostatic structural plasticity to stabilize sympathetic output, and how failures in this mechanism (as seen in SHR rats) lead to pathological hyperactivity. This branch connects Sapolsky's compassion argument to concrete developmental and homeostatic mechanisms.

### Emergent Agency and Top-Down Causation
*Kevin Mitchell's counter-case: organisms are organized systems whose goals constrain their parts.* Higher-level organismic states — hunger, threat, curiosity, reproductive strategy, social meaning — can be real causes without violating physics. Eve Marder's work on thermal compensation [[../raw/marder-crab-thermal-compensation-homeostasis.md]] and Alon Korngreen's reassessment of the Hodgkin-Huxley model [[../raw/korngreen-population-modeling-hh.md]] provide concrete neural mechanisms for this: circuits maintain global goals (like escape responses or motor rhythms) by dynamically adjusting across degenerate parameter sets.

### Animal Agency Experiments
*Agency is visible below the human level in self-initiated movement, goal-directed action, planning, and habit switching.* Rat readiness signals, outcome devaluation, hippocampal vicarious trial-and-error, basal-ganglia manipulations, and research in [Invertebrate Neuroscience](./neuroscience/invertebrates/README.md) (thermal compensation, active sampling) give the debate an experimental backbone. This extends to human perceptual sampling, where longitudinal drift in gaze patterns is sufficient to account for observed representational drift in hierarchical visual models [[./neuroscience/computation/perceptual_sampling_drift_2026.md]].

### Niche Construction and Ecological Choice
*Organisms do not merely respond to environments; they modify the environments that later constrain them.* This widens agency from momentary decision to long-horizon feedback between organism and world.

### Homeostatic Arousal and Physiological State
*How the brain monitors internal needs and threats to regulate the conscious state.* This branch connects molecular sensors (like Cx26) to global state shifts (arousal), and mean-field dynamics (low-rank networks + adaptation) to macro-states like sleep, wakefulness, and anesthesia [[../raw/adaptation-states-sleep-wake.md]], showing how agency is constrained by the physiological requirements of the organism. Recent research into **metabolic psychiatry** [[./neuroscience/clinical/schizophrenia_transcriptomic_collapse_2026.md]] further extends this, identifying the HCAR2/HCAR1 tandem locus as a "master thermodynamic governor" whose transcriptomic collapse leads to a fuel-mismatch-driven crash in Schizophrenia, reframing psychiatric crisis as a failure of homeostatic energy sensing.
