# Invertebrate Neuroscience and Agency

This cluster tracks research on invertebrate neural circuits that provide evidence for autonomous agency, goal-directed behavior, and homeostatic control.

## Context
Invertebrate models offer circuit-level access to the mechanisms of agency, allowing us to see how "intent" and "policy" are implemented without the complexity of vertebrate cortical layers.
