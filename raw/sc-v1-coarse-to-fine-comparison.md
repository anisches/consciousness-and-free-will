# Superior Colliculus vs. V1 in Coarse-to-Fine Processing

## Finding
While both the superior colliculus (SC) and primary visual cortex (V1) exhibit coarse-to-fine (C2F) dynamics, these dynamics are significantly stronger and more prominent in the SC.

## Latency Drivers
- **Superior Colliculus**: Response latencies are dictated primarily by image spatial frequency (coarse patterns are faster), independent of the neurons' actual tuning curves.
- **Primary Visual Cortex**: Response latencies are largely dominated by visual response sensitivity, which is much more broadband than in the SC.

## Conclusion
C2F visual image processing is more of a collicular than a cortical phenomenon, suggesting it is highly optimized for driving foveating eye movements rather than general image inheritance from V1.
