# Robert Sapolsky on Free Will (Summary from Research)

## Core Arguments from 'Determined' (2023)

### 1. The Chain of Causality
Every human action is the result of an unbroken chain of causes:
- Seconds before: Neural firing patterns.
- Minutes to hours before: Hormones (stress, hunger).
- Days to months before: Neuroplasticity and life events.
- Years before: Childhood/epigenetics.
- Centuries before: Evolutionary/cultural history.

### 2. Critique of Compatibilism
Sapolsky rejects the idea that free will can coexist with determinism. If "choices" are products of prior causes, they aren't free.

### 3. Societal Implications
- Justice: Move to a "quarantine" model (treating dangerous people like malfunctioning machines, no moral blame).
- Meritocracy: Success is "biological luck."
- Compassion: Radical empathy from realizing no one chose their brain.

## Frontiers and Anomalies
- The Replication Crisis: reliance on "Hungry Judge" study which is being re-analyzed.
- Top-Down Causation: Kevin Mitchell (Free Agents) argues organisms have intrinsic causal power.
- Superdeterminism: Sabine Hossenfelder suggests everything fixed at Big Bang.
