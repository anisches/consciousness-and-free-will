# Transcriptomic Double Dissociation of Lactate and BHB Sensors in SCZ

**Tags:** #neuroscience #genomics #schizophrenia #metabolism #lactate #BHB

Schizophrenia is characterized by a massive transcriptomic "double dissociation" driven by structural collapse flanking the **HCAR2/HCAR1** locus:

1.  **HCAR1 Downregulation:** Loss of the "lactate emergency brake," leading to unregulated lactate dynamics.
2.  **HCAR2 Paralysis:** Inability of the BHB/niacin "cooling switch" to function, preventing metabolic stabilization via ketone bodies.

This genomic etiology provides a unified explanation for historical SCZ biomarkers related to metabolic dysfunction and oxidative stress. It identifies the HCAR locus as a primary site of pathology where genomic structural variance translates into functional metabolic failure.
