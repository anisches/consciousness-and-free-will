# Sapolsky's Causal Cascade and Biological Determinism

## Core thesis

Sapolsky's hard determinism is strongest when read as a multi-scale causal cascade. Behavior is shaped by seconds-before neural activity, minutes-to-hours hormonal state, days-to-years plasticity and trauma, lifetime development, culture, evolution, and contingency. There is no clean inner executive standing outside the cascade.

## Why it matters

- It attacks meritocracy and retributive blame at their biological root.
- It reframes "character" as a history of causes sedimented into a nervous system.
- It pushes justice toward prevention, containment, rehabilitation, and compassion rather than desert.

## Counterpoint

Causal explanation does not automatically show that agency is unreal. Mitchell's response is that the organism itself, as an organized whole, can be part of the causal explanation.
