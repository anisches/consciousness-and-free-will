# Attractors, Metastability, and Agency

## Core thesis

Brains can be modeled as dynamical systems moving through state spaces shaped by attractors, basins, transitions, and metastable regimes. Habits, moods, identities, compulsions, and skills may correspond to stable or semi-stable regions of the neural landscape. Agency becomes the capacity to alter transitions, escape basins, or reshape the landscape over time.

## Why it matters

- Habits are not merely repeated choices; they are stabilized trajectories.
- Deliberation may occur near unstable boundaries between action regimes.
- Rumination and addiction can be modeled as pathological attractor traps.
- Learning and therapy can be interpreted as landscape reshaping.

## Counterpoint

Attractor language is powerful but can become metaphorical. It should be grounded in measured neural population dynamics where possible.
