# Gromov-Wasserstein Optimal Transport (GWOT) in Neurogeometry

## Concept
Gromov-Wasserstein Optimal Transport (GWOT) is an unsupervised method for comparing the representational structure of two different spaces (e.g., human neural activity and DNN embeddings) without needing to align individual data points beforehand.

## Application in Perception
In the study of color perception, GWOT allows researchers to measure the "structural congruence" between:
1.  **Human Psychophysical Data**: Similarity judgments of color pairs.
2.  **Model Embeddings**: Vector representations in DNN layers.

## Advantages over RSA
Unlike Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA), which typically requires the same set of stimuli in both systems, GWOT can compare spaces with different dimensionalities and even different sets of items by finding the optimal "coupling" that preserves the global geometric structure (distances).
