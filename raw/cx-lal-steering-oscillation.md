# CX-LAL Interaction in Insect Steering

## Concept
The emergence of scanning behaviors in insects can be explained by the interaction between the Central Complex (CX) steering circuits and Lateral Accessory Lobe (LAL) oscillators.

## Details
This biologically grounded model demonstrates that:
1.  **CX Steering:** Provides goal-directed orientation.
2.  **LAL Oscillators:** Generate rhythmic sampling movements.
3.  **Integration:** The combined system produces diverse scan dynamics (saccades, fixations) without requiring a specialized "scanning module." This suggests a distributed control principle where complex behaviors emerge from the coupling of simpler, conserved circuits.
