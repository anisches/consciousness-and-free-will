# Flyvis Connectome-Constrained Geometry
In the Drosophila visual system, connectome constraints are necessary to replicate the smooth circular direction geometry seen in biological T4/T5 neurons. 

Networks constrained by the Flyvis connectome achieve significantly higher alignment with biological data (r = 0.930) compared to stability-constrained random baselines (r = 0.603). This confirms that biological wiring contributes specific representational structures (e.g., circular direction geometry) that are not emergent from general stability constraints or random sign-preserving weight shuffles.
