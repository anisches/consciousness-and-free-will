# Attenuated Glial Response in Spontaneously Hypertensive Rats (SHR)

**Tags:** #hypertension #SHR #clinical-neuroscience #plasticity-defects

In neonatal Spontaneously Hypertensive Rats (SHR), which exhibit early SN hyperactivity and the eventual development of hypertension, the homeostatic response is impaired. 

SHR SNs show an **attenuated response to glia** during both initial synapse formation and activity-dependent plasticity. This failure of the SGC-mediated homeostatic mechanism may contribute to the persistent SN hyperactivity that drives the development of hypertension in this model. This highlights how failures in homeostatic "brakes" (mediated by glia) can lead to pathological states like chronic hypertension.
