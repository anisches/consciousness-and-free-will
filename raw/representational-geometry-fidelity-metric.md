# Representational Geometry as a Fidelity Metric
Representational geometry — the structure of pairwise distances between population responses to different stimuli — provides a robust population-level metric for biological fidelity. 

Unlike behavioral decoders, representational geometry captures how a neural circuit organizes its representational space. It can discriminate between real biological wiring and arbitrary wiring by comparing the representational similarity analysis (RSA) or centered kernel alignment (CKA) of models against biological data.
