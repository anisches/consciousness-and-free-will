# Animal Experiments in Agency and Volition

## Key Studies
1. **Self-Initiated Action Timing** (Murakami 2014): Predictable neural antecedents in rats.
2. **Evidence Accumulation** (Shadlen 2001): Decision as accumulation in LIP.
3. **Goal-Directed vs Habit** (Dickinson 1981): Outcome devaluation in rats.
4. **Deliberation/Future Simulation** (Johnson 2007): Hippocampal 'vicarious trial and error'.
5. **Causal Manipulation** (Tai 2012; Kravitz 2010): Striatal/Basal Ganglia circuit control.
