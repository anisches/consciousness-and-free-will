# Animal Experiments in Agency and Volition

## Key Studies
1. **Self-Initiated Action Timing** (Murakami 2014): Predictable neural antecedents in rats.
2. **Evidence Accumulation** (Shadlen 2001): Decision as accumulation in LIP.
3. **Goal-Directed vs Habit** (Dickinson 1981): Outcome devaluation in rats.
4. **Deliberation/Future Simulation** (Johnson 2007): Hippocampal 'vicarious trial and error'.
5. **Causal Manipulation** (Tai 2012; Kravitz 2010): Striatal/Basal Ganglia circuit control.
6. **Homeostatic Compensation** (Marder 2026): Thermal robustness in crab motor systems; agency as self-maintenance. [[raw/marder-crab-thermal-compensation-homeostasis.md]]
7. **Coarse-to-Fine Visual Dynamics** (Hafed 2026): Superior Colliculus prioritizes coarse information for foveating eye movements; stronger than V1. [[biology/neuroscience/anatomy/primate_sc_v1_visual_processing_2026.md]]
8. **Hypercapnic Arousal** (Huang 2026): Cx26 hemichannels as CO2 sensors in DR-VTA arousal circuit. [[raw/huang-co2-arousal-cx26.md]]
9. **Active Sampling and Scanning** (Sun 2026): Emergent exploration/exploitation behaviors from conserved insect neural circuits. [[biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/ant_scanning_circuits_2026.md]]
