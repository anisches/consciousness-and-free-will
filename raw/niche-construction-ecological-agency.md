# Niche Construction and Ecological Agency

## Core thesis

Organisms do not merely adapt to environments; they alter environments that then feed back into future selection and behavior. Niche construction expands agency beyond momentary choice into long-horizon world-shaping.

## Why it matters for free will

- Agency includes changing the constraints that later constrain you.
- Human institutions, tools, habits, and built environments become externalized control systems.
- This bridges biology and culture: the agent is partly made by a world it helps remake.

## Counterpoint

Niche construction is not proof of contra-causal freedom. It is a feedback model: caused organisms become causes of future conditions.
