# Satellite Glial Cells Drive Homeostatic Synaptic Structural Plasticity in Sympathetic Neurons

**Tags:** #neuroscience #plasticity #homeostasis #glia #PNS

Harrison et al. (2026) demonstrate that Sympathetic Neurons (SNs) exhibit homeostatic synaptic structural plasticity in response to long-term activity changes. This compensatory shift in synaptic density is bidirectional: hyperactivity leads to downregulation and hypoactivity to upregulation. 

Critically, this plasticity is mediated by **Satellite Glial Cells (SGCs)**. In the absence of SGCs, SNs fail to exhibit homeostatic plasticity, and direct chemogenetic activation of SGCs is sufficient to drive these compensatory changes even in the absence of neuronal activity changes. This identifies SGCs as the primary drivers of circuit stability in the peripheral sympathetic nervous system.
