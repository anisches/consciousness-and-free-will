# Physics and Topology Expansion Plan: Quantum, Time, Manifolds, and Agency

Scope: deepen `physics/README.md` and `topology/README.md` around quantum indeterminacy, decoherence, the block universe, superdeterminism, causal structure, neural manifolds, attractors, metastability, and embodied state-space geometry.

## Key claims to add

1. **Indeterminacy is not agency.** Quantum randomness can break classical determinism, but a random twitch is not a responsible choice unless embedded in control architecture.
2. **The physics debate often turns on independence assumptions.** Bell experiments, the Free Will Theorem, and superdeterminism all expose how physics uses assumptions about measurement settings, interventions, and causal independence.
3. **The block universe does not trivially kill agency.** Eternalism can make deliberation look fixed from outside spacetime while still being a real causal process inside the worldline.
4. **Topology gives agency a shape.** Neural and cognitive systems have constrained state spaces; choices are trajectories, attractor transitions, basin escapes, and policy-space navigations.
5. **Metastability is the geometry of hesitation.** Brains can hover between regimes; deliberation may be the lived correlate of unstable or metastable dynamics before commitment.

## Critiques / counterpoints to include

- **Quantum randomness is not self-authorship.** A randomizer in the skull would not improve responsibility unless organized by reasons, control, and feedback.
- **Orch-OR is speculative and contested.** It belongs as a frontier hypothesis, not as established neuroscience.
- **Superdeterminism has explanatory costs.** It can preserve determinism but threatens the independence assumptions that make experiments interpretable.
- **Geometry can become metaphorical overreach.** Neural manifolds are empirically useful for population dynamics; mapping them directly to qualia requires extra argument.
- **Attractors can explain both agency and bondage.** Stable basins support identity and skill, but also addiction, trauma loops, and compulsive rumination.
