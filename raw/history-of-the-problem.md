# History of the Problem of Free Will

## Ancient Framing
- Focus: responsibility, virtue, compulsion, habit.
- Anchor: Aristotle (voluntary vs involuntary).

## Theological/Moral
- Coexistence of responsibility with divine foreknowledge.
- Anchors: Augustine, Aquinas.

## Early Modern
- Mechanistic science pressure.
- Anchors: Descartes, Hobbes, Spinoza, Leibniz, Hume.

## Enlightenment
- Autonomy and practical reason.
- Anchor: Kant.

## Scientific/Psychological
- Unconscious processing and evolution.
- Anchors: James, Freud, behaviorism, Libet.
