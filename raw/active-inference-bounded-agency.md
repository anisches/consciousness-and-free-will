# Active Inference and Bounded Agency

## Core thesis

Active inference and predictive processing model organisms as systems that reduce uncertainty by perceiving, acting, and selecting policies. Bounded rationality adds the missing realism: agents do not optimize with infinite compute; they satisfice under constraints.

## Agency model

- The agent carries a generative model of the world.
- Perception updates beliefs; action changes sensory input.
- Policy selection minimizes expected surprise/free energy.
- Precision weighting, affect, attention, and bodily state change what policies are available.
- Bounded rationality makes agency finite, heuristic, and situated.

## Why it matters for free will

This is the strongest computational compatibilist branch: "will" becomes controlled policy selection under uncertainty rather than uncaused metaphysical sparkle.

## Counterpoint

Free-energy language can become too broad. It must be tied to concrete mechanisms: policy selection, error minimization, precision weighting, action-perception loops, and resource bounds.
