# Neo: The Metaphor of Distributed Agency

## Core Concept
Agency as a hierarchical, distributed system rather than a singular "voice" or "chooser."
- **Local Evaluators**: Ganglia/subordinate processes (texture, salience, affordance).
- **Global Controller**: Integration and policy-making.

## Layers of Agency
1. Local Evaluation (Sensory/Predicted)
2. Salience (Routing)
3. Global Context (Needs)
4. Policy (Allowed/Forbidden)
5. Action
6. Explanation (Storytelling)
