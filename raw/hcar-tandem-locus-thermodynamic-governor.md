# HCAR1/HCAR2 Tandem Locus as the Brain's Master Thermodynamic Governor

**Tags:** #neuroscience #metabolism #homeostasis #HCAR1 #HCAR2 #thermodynamics

The **HCAR2/HCAR1 tandem locus** is identified as the brain's master thermodynamic governor (2026-07-01 study). This genomic region acts as a metabolic sensor array that regulates the brain's energy state by sensing key fuel molecules.

- **HCAR1 (Lactate Receptor):** Acts as the "lactate emergency brake."
- **HCAR2 (BHB/Niacin Receptor):** Acts as the beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) and niacin "cooling switch."

The coordination of these two receptors ensures thermodynamic stability in the hominid brain. Structural variances flanking this locus are linked to severe transcriptomic collapse, particularly in psychiatric contexts like Schizophrenia.
