# Consciousness and Free Will Knowledge Brain

A curated research repository and Zettelkasten slip-box on the nature of mind, choice, and agency.

## Central Tension

The repo is organized around a single live argument:

> If every choice has causes, what — if anything — remains of agency?

The answer is not settled by shouting "determinism" or "freedom." Each branch tests a different pressure point: mathematical predictability, biological constraint, algorithmic control, physical openness, and the geometry of experience.

## Knowledge Domains

### [Mathematical Determinism](math/README.md)
*Is the universe a giant equation? Gödel, chaos, computational irreducibility, probability, and the gap between determination and prediction.*

### [Algorithmic Free Will](algo/README.md)
*Mind as a computational control system: accumulator models, veto loops, active inference, bounded rationality, sandboxes, and recursive self-modeling.*

### [Topology & Geometry of Experience](topology/README.md)
*Mapping qualia and agency onto neural manifolds, attractor basins, metastable dynamics, affordance spaces, and representational alignment (GWOT).*

### [Quantum Mechanics & Time](physics/README.md)
*Indeterminacy, decoherence, the Free Will Theorem, superdeterminism, block-universe time, and whether physics leaves room for causal agency.*

### [Biological Agency](biology/README.md)
*Sapolsky's causal cascade, stress/allostasis, development, Mitchell's emergent agency, animal experiments (active sampling, thermal compensation), and physiological arousal pathways (Cx26).*

## Recent Research (2026)

The repository was updated in June and July 2026 with findings on:
- **Neural Manifolds & Color**: Representational alignment between humans and DNNs using GWOT [[biology/neuroscience/computation/color_perception_dnn_2026.md]].
- **Superior Colliculus Dynamics**: Coarse-to-fine visual processing prioritization in the SC [[biology/neuroscience/anatomy/primate_sc_v1_visual_processing_2026.md]].
- **Invertebrate Agency**: Thermal robustness in crabs [[raw/marder-crab-thermal-compensation-homeostasis.md]] and active sampling in desert ants [[biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/ant_scanning_circuits_2026.md]].
- **Physiological State**: CO2-sensitive arousal pathways (Cx26) and homeostatic constraints on agency [[raw/huang-co2-arousal-cx26.md]].
- **Mean-Field Oscillations**: How low-rank structure and adaptation produce rich oscillatory dynamics (Up-Down states, chaos) in recurrent networks [[raw/zheng-low-rank-adaptation-oscillations.md]].
- **Spectral Neural Dynamics**: Closed functional equations for non-normal RNN covariance spectra [[raw/zavatone-veth-nonnormal-rnn-covariance.md]] and the discrete-continuous time analytical divergence [[raw/non-normal-discrete-continuous-divergence.md]].
- **Glial-Mediated Homeostasis**: How satellite glial cells (SGCs) drive bidirectional homeostatic structural plasticity in the PNS to stabilize sympathetic output [[raw/harrison-2026-sgc-homeostatic-plasticity.md]].
- **Representational Fidelity**: Using representational geometry to discriminate biological wiring from arbitrary wiring in the Drosophila visual system [[biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/zhou_flyvis_geometry_2026.md]].
- **Whole-Brain Connectivity**: Conserved linear scaling of structure-function relationships in Drosophila (matching human patterns) and the outsized impact of unsegregated synapses on functional connectivity [[biology/neuroscience/invertebrates/okuno_drosophila_2025.md]].
- **Parameter Degeneracy**: Rethinking Hodgkin-Huxley robustness; how population-based modeling reveals that canonical parameter sets are a minority subpopulation compared to robust phasic firing modes [[raw/hh-parameter-degeneracy-robustness.md]].
- **Metabolic Psychiatry**: Discovery of the HCAR2/HCAR1 "master thermodynamic governor" and its transcriptomic collapse in Schizophrenia, reframing the pathology as a diet-driven "fuel mismatch" and thermodynamic crash [[biology/neuroscience/clinical/schizophrenia_transcriptomic_collapse_2026.md]].
- **Perceptual Sampling & Drift**: Evidence that representational drift in the visual cortex is driven by systematic shifts in gaze patterns (active sampling) over time, rather than just intrinsic neural instability [[biology/neuroscience/computation/perceptual_sampling_drift_2026.md]].
- **Cross-Scale Manifold Analysis**: Introduction of the **DRIADA toolkit**, demonstrating that selectivity-based filtering can restore latent embeddings and that ~57% of manifold-informative neurons lack classical behavioral selectivity [[biology/neuroscience/computation/pospelov_driada_toolkit_2026.md]].
- **Virtual Brain Twins**: Introduction of **Cohort-Amortized Personalization (CAP)** and **CrossCoder**, enabling fast, private, and atlas-independent personalization of generative brain models through model-sharing and synthetic access [[biology/neuroscience/computation/esmaeili_cap_brain_twins_2026.md]].
- **Unified Brain Dynamics**: Unification of connectome harmonics, turbulence, and complex harmonics (CHARM) under a single self-adjoint operator—the Connectome Laplacian—revealing that structural perturbations (e.g., LSD) move all functional signatures in unison [[topology/kringelbach_unified_operator_2026.md]].
