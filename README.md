# Consciousness and Free Will

A research notebook on consciousness, agency, volition, and the free-will debate.

The project starts from primitives: the historical questions, the major positions, and the experimental results that shaped the debate. The goal is not to defend a view early. The first pass is classification, source collection, and conceptual hygiene.

## Working Questions

- What is consciousness: experience, access, reportability, integration, self-modeling, or something else?
- What is free will: metaphysical freedom, practical agency, moral responsibility, control, or authorship?
- Which parts of the debate are philosophical, neuroscientific, psychological, computational, or semantic?
- Which thought experiments clarify the problem, and which only rename it?
- What do neuro results actually show about agency and conscious intention?
